## Takeaways

- **GROBID:** gives usable sections, but headings go wrong (run-in titles promoted, numbering merged, table headers as sections). The Abstract is polluted by figure captions in 2 of 5 papers. Equations are dropped and tables become number runs.
- **PyMuPDF:** keeps all body text and equation symbols, but with no structure. Equations are scrambled and table numbers split one per line. The References cut silently drops appendices and supplements.
- **Next steps:** use GROBID for section-level chunking. Fall back to the arXiv abstract for the Abstract, and skip sections that are mostly numbers. Fix PyMuPDF's reference cut if you keep it as a fallback.
