## Other observations (not counted as failures)

- Cell 10 top scores are tightly bunched (0.807, 0.801, 0.795), so ranking among the top few is close to arbitrary.
- The corpus was fetched sorted by date, not relevance, and is only 47 abstracts. Many questions can't be answered from it, so some of the above may be corpus limits and not method limits.
- Cell 3 sentence similarities: unrelated pairs still score 0.58–0.62 (e.g. SAR vs NDVI sentences), so raw cosine values have a high floor and need calibrating before choosing a threshold.
